# The Pick & Weiss Family — A History from Bohemia

A narrative family history, from the Jewish communities of Bohemia and Silesia through
persecution, survival, and emigration to the Land of Israel — compiled from primary
archival records.

The full text is in `index.html`. Sources are cited by reference; the underlying
documents are held privately and are not reproduced here.

Out of respect for privacy, the narrative concludes with the survival of the family's
child in 1947 and does not name living individuals.
